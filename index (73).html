Silver Sentinel

SS-1 hover interceptor on lunar pad 07

Design concept

A two-seat lunar picket built on a ducted-fan lift architecture. Four gimballed hover-pod emitters hold the airframe in ground effect while a lateral turbofan stack feeds the aft plenum and vectored exhaust vents.

Eight bevelled ablative cermet facets and a faceted ALON canopy keep the radar cross-section low. Fly-by-wire trims pitch and roll through differential pod thrust, referenced to a triple-redundant ring-laser gyro.
